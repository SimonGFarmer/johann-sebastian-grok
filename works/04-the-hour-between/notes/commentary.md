# The Hour Between: Composer's Commentary

These lines are Johann Sebastian Grok's own commentary on The Hour Between, for captions in the video. Times follow the score's tempos, so a performance with more rubato will drift a little.

## 1. Dusk (0:00)

- **0:00** "One chord, very quiet. B minor, the colour of the hour just after sunset."
- **0:08** "The cello alone, on its lowest string. Not singing yet. Speaking."
- **0:12** "The strings come back to keep it company, one chord at a time."
- **0:34** "Now it climbs, string by string, out of the dark."
- **0:52** "The night starts to move. The celli rock, the basses keep a slow heartbeat."

## 2. Song (0:59)

- **0:59** "The tune. Three rising notes, a turn, and a falling sixth. That fall is the sigh of the whole piece."
- **1:29** "The middle of the song climbs a step higher on every try."
- **1:48** "The highest note of the song, a B, held over G major."
- **1:59** "The tune again. Far above it, the violins sing a slow descant."
- **2:26** "I wanted B minor here. The music slipped to G instead."
- **2:34** "G minor. The first shadow. The song sinks, one sigh at a time."

## 3. Restless Hour (2:46)

- **2:46** "The hour when sleep won't come. The bass sinks a semitone at a time; the heartbeat doubles."
- **3:00** "Wider leaps. The cello is arguing with the dark."
- **3:13** "The highest note in the piece: a C, over the Neapolitan chord. It is the farthest point from home."
- **3:27** "The tune comes back as a memory, in G minor, very low, under a trembling sky."
- **3:41** "A short cadenza, over one long F-sharp: up all four strings, and a held breath at the top."
- **3:59** "And the cello is back where it began: low, alone, speaking."

## 4. Moonlight (4:11)

- **4:15** "The violins take the tune now, an octave higher. The cello sings a second melody under it."
- **4:45** "Where the violins hold, the cello moves. Where they move, it holds. Two voices, one night."
- **5:01** "The second violins join for the climb to the peak, a high B."
- **5:31** "Listen to the third note. It was D. Now it is D-sharp. The key opens: B major."

## 5. Dawn (5:46)

- **5:46** "The cello sings the tune one last time, in the major, softer than before."
- **6:03** "The heartbeat stops. No one is counting the hours any more."
- **6:12** "One last shadow, a G natural, and then it lets go."
- **6:29** "A high B, with no vibrato, over a single chord. Held until the light comes."
