THEMES AND MOTIFS  (SGN notation, see 00-format.txt; concert pitch)
=====================================================================
Every theme of the symphony grows from the CELL. Interval content:
 a = repeated note (drive)   b = rising minor 3rd (lament / defiance)
 c = stepwise fall through the leading tone and back (the "turn")
 d = falling 4th/5th to the dominant (the "kick" that restarts the bar)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE CELL (Mvt I ostinato, vc+cb), one bar 4/4, always staccato
 CELL(g)   | 8 g2' g2' bb2' a2' g2' f#2' g2' d2' |
 transpositions used in I (same shape, real leading tone):
 CELL(c)   | 8 c3' c3' eb3' d3' c3' b2' c3' g2' |
 CELL(d)   | 8 d3' d3' f#3' e3' d3' c#3' d3' a2' |   (dominant, major 3rd)
 CELL(eb)  | 8 eb3' eb3' g3' f3' eb3' d3' eb3' bb2' |
 CELL(bb)  | 8 bb2' bb2' d3' c3' bb2' a2' bb2' f2' |
 CELL(f)   | 8 f2' f2' a2' g2' f2' e2' f2' c2' |
 CELL' (modal variant, whole-step "turn", used for subdominant colour)
 CELL'(c)  | 8 c3' c3' eb3' d3' c3' bb2' c3' g2' |
 CELL-MAJOR (2nd group / Trio / finale apotheosis)
 CELLM(bb) | 8 bb2' bb2' d3' c3' bb2' a2' bb2' f2' |  (= CELL(bb))
 CELLM(g)  | 8 g2' g2' b2' a2' g2' f#2' g2' d2' |
 CADENTIAL CELL (V-i in one bar)
 CELLcad   | 8 d3' d3' a2' f#2' g2' g2' bb2' d2' |
 INTENSITY VARIANTS (see L-scale in 01-plan):
 L3        | 16 g2>' g2' g2' g2' 8 bb2' a2' g2' f#2' g2' d2' |
 L5        | 16 g2^ g2' g2' g2' 8 bb2' a2' 16 g2^ g2' g2' g2' 8 g2' d2' |
 L1 broken | 8 g2' r bb2' a2' g2' r g2' d2' |
 DIMINUTION (16ths, violins, transition & finale)
 CELL16(g) | 16 g5' g5' bb5' a5' g5' f#5' g5' d5' (half bar) |
 AUGMENTATION (quarters, Mvt II horns, Mvt IV development)
 CELL4(eb) | 4 eb4 eb4 g4 f4 | 4 eb4 d4 eb4 bb3 |

CROSS-MOVEMENT LINKS
 - CELL: I bass ostinato -> II walking pizz bass (major) + storm in B
   section (literal I ostinato) -> III scherzo melody / trio major ->
   IV motto, P theme opening, and the major apotheosis in the coda.
 - rising 6th (T2 of I) = head of II cantilena = S of IV.
 - T1 head (rising 4th + turn) recalled at the climax of IV development.
 - G major ending of IV answers the minor CELL with CELLM(g).
