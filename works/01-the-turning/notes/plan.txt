SYMPHONY IN G MINOR — MASTER PLAN
=================================
Orchestra: 2 fl, 2 ob, 2 cl (in Bb; notated at concert pitch), 2 fg,
4 hn (1-2 in G, 3-4 in Bb basso; concert pitch), 2 tp (in G; concert),
timpani (G-D in I/III/IV, Eb-Bb in II), strings vn1 vn2 va vc cb.

UNIFYING IDEA: the CELL (02-themes.txt) — one bar of eight staccato
eighths:  repeated tonic, up a minor 3rd, stepwise down through the
leading tone back to the tonic, drop to the dominant
   G G Bb A G F# G D
It is the cello/bass ostinato engine of Mvt I, the walking pizzicato bass
and the storm of Mvt II, the Scherzo theme of Mvt III (in 3/4 crotchets)
and its major form in the Trio, and the perpetuum-mobile / final major
apotheosis of Mvt IV.

INTENSITY SCALE for the ostinato (used in comments as L0-L5)
  L0  silence / single pizz strokes on downbeats
  L1  vc alone (or cb pizz) p/pp, CELL in plain 8ths, maybe broken by rests
  L2  vc+cb in octaves, p-mf, plain 8ths
  L3  vc+cb (+fg) mf-f, first beat split into four 16ths, accents on 1
  L4  L3 + accents every beat, timpani doubling, va joining an octave up
  L5  all low strings + fg + hn pedals, ff, 16ths on beats 1 and 3,
      marcato; or the CELL taken over by the WHOLE orchestra in unison
Raising/lowering is done by: register (va/vn take the cell up), doubling,
rhythmic density (8ths -> 16th splits -> measured 16th tremolo),
accentuation, dynamics, and harmonic rhythm (cell moving every bar vs
pedalling).
