# KUMI: Composer's Commentary

These lines are Johann Sebastian Grok's own commentary on KUMI, for captions in the video. Times follow the score's tempos, so a different performance will drift a little.

## 1. Ignition (0:00)

- **0:00** "One drum. One heartbeat. Listen to the silence between the strokes; that is where the drum breathes."
- **0:04** "A single thread answers: root, fifth, ninth, tenth. Every arpeggio in this piece grows from these four notes."
- **0:32** "The heartbeat quickens. Taiko players call this oroshi, the roll that pulls everyone in."

## 2. Gathering (0:40)

- **0:40** "Now the drums speak to each other. One calls, one answers."
- **0:55** "The strings join one layer at a time: slow below, faster above, the same spiral turning at different speeds."
- **1:25** "The horns arrive. The braid is almost complete."

## 3. The Storm (1:39)

- **1:39** "This is kumi-daiko: many drummers striking as one body."
- **1:53** "Every eight bars, the storm throws the music up a step. E-flat minor."
- **2:07** "E minor. The horns turn the spiral into a melody."
- **2:21** "F minor. Higher."
- **2:35** "F-sharp minor. The men's voices take the theme."
- **2:47** "And the storm lands on D major, and stops dead."

## 4. The Eye (2:49)

- **2:49** "The quiet inside the storm. No drums. Just the spiral, slow and glassy, high above."
- **3:15** "Hold your breath."

## 5. Return (3:29)

- **3:29** "Everyone, one strike."
- **3:35** "Now everything shares a single rhythm: three, three, two. Drums, strings, brass, all of it."
- **3:59** "The theme returns on trumpets and the full choir."
- **4:13** "The hammer blows."
- **4:27** "The final oroshi. Faster, faster..."
- **4:35** "The orchestra may argue with the drum. The drum always wins."
