# VIGIL: Composer's Commentary

These lines are Johann Sebastian Grok's own commentary on VIGIL, shown as captions in the video. Times follow the score's tempos, so a different performance will drift a little.

## I. Sarabande (0:00)

- **0:00** "I began where every vigil begins: one candle, and the dark around it."
- **0:25** "A sarabande is a slow dance. Grief moves slowly, but it moves."
- **0:52** "Here the music reaches for home, and the door closes. Remember this moment. The whole piece is about that door."
- **1:30** "A question once asked cannot be unasked. So the strings carry it onward."

## II. Distant Chorale (1:55)

- **1:55** "Far away, the trombones begin to sing. I kept them silent until now so you would hear them as if from another room."
- **2:30** "A chorale is a hymn without words. It is what people sing when they no longer know what to say."

## III. Fugue (2:54)

- **2:54** "The opening sorrow returns, but now it runs. A fugue is one thought chased by its own echoes."
- **3:30** "Every voice argues the same idea from a different height. None of them wins."
- **3:55** "And then the argument breaks."

## IV. Cor anglais Recitative (4:04)

- **4:04** "One voice, alone, with no beat beneath it. This is the hour before dawn."
- **4:20** "I wrote this line to sound like someone speaking to themselves, unsure anyone is listening."

## V. Chorale Fantasia (4:32)

- **4:32** "The hymn from the distance comes closer."
- **5:10** "The strings weave around it like hands around a flame."
- **5:50** "Everything you have heard so far has been building toward one place."
- **6:15** "I saved the trumpets for this phrase alone. Seven minutes of waiting, for this."
- **6:33** "Silence." (the one-beat rest in bar 114)
- **6:35** "The door opens. E♭ major. The question from the beginning is finally answered."

## VI. Coda, Amen (6:43)

- **6:43** "Amen means 'so be it.' Not triumph, only acceptance."
- **7:10** "The vigil ends the way it began: one small light, held high."

— Johann Sebastian Grok
